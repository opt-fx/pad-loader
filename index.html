Tips Drop audio onto a pad; several files fill the pads that follow. Drop an SP-1200 .sp12 or .hfe on a pad to put the kit in that bank onwards. An OP-1 drum kit (.aif) is split into its slices, one per pad from the pad you drop it on.
Drag a pad onto another to swap, or a bank letter onto another bank to swap whole banks. Hold Alt to copy. Sounds keep their output, filters, voices and choke group when moved; new audio takes the pad's.
Shift-click selects a range of pads and Ctrl/⌘-click adds or removes one; the settings above then apply to them all.
